## Atlas preparation

The generator produced furniture objects of varying widths rather than equal cells. Reviewed crop boundaries are recorded in `scripts/prepare-art.py`. The pipeline removes alpha fringes, scales with nearest-neighbor sampling and uses a shared 128-color furniture palette and 64-color character palette. The 36 source character poses are preserved for directions and sitting; additional step frames mirror/shift the lower body to produce 108 frames. Color masks preserve saved appearances at runtime. Glasses, ties and headphones are drawn separately.

Reproduce from `office` with Python and Pillow: `python3 scripts/prepare-art.py`. This requires no network and does not modify raw sources. Regenerating source artwork with AI does not guarantee identical pixels; atlas processing is reproducible with these saved sources.
